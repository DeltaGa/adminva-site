Delete your Cadence account

In the app

Settings, Account, Delete my account, then confirm.

Without the app

Write to joram@adminva.ca, subject “Cadence - account deletion”. Deletion is done within 30 days and confirmed by email.

What is deleted

Your personal information linked to Cadence: your term, what you told Cadence and your settings. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription: cancel it in your app store.

Your whole term. You speak, it follows.
