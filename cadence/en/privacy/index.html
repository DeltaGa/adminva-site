Privacy policy

Effective November 8, 2026. Cadence app, published by Joram Smith Tchicdje Kouojip, Quebec, Canada.

What Cadence handles

Your term (courses, timetable, evaluations, grades) and what you tell Cadence so it can help you, including the way you study. This information is used only to make Cadence work for you.

Your credentials

Your school access code and password stay on your phone; they are never sent to us.

Other calendars

If you connect another calendar, Cadence reads only what you choose and writes nothing to it. Cadence’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Pre-order

Payment for a pre-order is processed by Stripe. Your name, student ID and email are asked there, only to activate the month in your name and to refund you on request.

No sale, no advertising

We sell no information and share none for advertising. Cadence shows no ads.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information. You delete everything in the app or from the Account deletion page; for any request, write to joram@adminva.ca.

Age

Cadence is meant for college and university students and is not directed to children.

Changes

A significant change to this policy is announced in the app before it takes effect.
