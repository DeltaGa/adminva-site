Privacy policy

Effective November 7, 2026. Cadence mobile app, published by Joram Smith Tchicdje Kouojip (“we”), Quebec, Canada. The French version prevails.

Current test version. As long as the app only offers the sample term (no sign-in to a student account), Cadence collects and sends no personal information: everything stays in the app, on your phone. The rest of this policy describes the full service.

Cadence is a voice-driven term calendar for students. This policy says what information Cadence handles, why, where it goes and how to have it deleted.

Contact

Joram Smith Tchicdje Kouojip. For any question or any request to access, correct or delete your information: see the Support page.

What stays on your phone only

  • Your school access code and password, only if you choose “Save”: kept in the phone’s secure vault (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore). Never sent to our servers.
  • The key of your authenticator app, only if you scan it: same vault, same rule.
  • The sign-in cookies of your school: in the app, on the phone.
  • The access to your other calendars (Google, Microsoft), if you connect one: same vault, same rule.

Cadence signs in to your student account from your phone, with your own credentials, to read what your school already shows you: your timetable, your courses, your submissions, your published grades and your course outlines. Cadence never posts, changes or sends anything on your school’s sites.

Dictation is done by your phone’s speech recognition; Cadence receives the text, not the audio.

Your other calendars (optional)

When this feature is offered, you will be able to connect a Google calendar, a Microsoft calendar (Outlook, Microsoft 365) or a subscription link. Cadence only reads them, and only the calendars you choose; it never writes to them. Of the events you choose to import, we keep the time, the title and the calendar they come from, to count your free time and prepare your evening plan. “Disconnect” erases the access and the imported events.

Cadence’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Providers that process information for us

These providers may process information outside Quebec. We send them only what is needed, and they may not use it for any other purpose.

  • Artificial intelligence: to understand your commands (TypeSafe) and to read course outlines (a language-model provider). They receive the text of your commands and the part of your term needed for the answer, or the text of a course outline. This requires your explicit consent in the app; without it, voice commands and outline reading are turned off.
  • Hosting and network: Cloudflare (routing), our server.
  • Notifications: Expo, Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging), Apple (APNs).
  • Subscriptions: RevenueCat, Google Play, App Store. We never see your payment method.
  • Crashes: Sentry.

We sell no information and share none for advertising. Cadence shows no ads.

How long

  • Your term data: as long as your account exists, then 30 days after a subscription ends, then deleted.
  • Course outlines and what is extracted from them: up to one year after the end of the term.
  • Technical logs: 30 days, with no personal content.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, port or delete your information, and withdraw your consent at any time. Full deletion is done in the app (Settings, Account, Delete my account) or through the Account deletion page. You may also file a complaint with the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.

Security

Exchanges are encrypted (HTTPS). Your school credentials never leave your phone. If a privacy incident presents a serious risk, we notify you.

Age

Cadence is meant for college and university students and is not directed at children.

Schools

Cadence is an independent app, not affiliated with your school.

This site

This site sets no cookies, does not measure visits and loads nothing from another site.

Changes

A significant change to this policy is announced in the app before it takes effect.
