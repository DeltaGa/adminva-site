Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026. Cadence app, published by Joram Smith Tchicdje Kouojip (Cadence Session on Google Play), Quebec, Canada.

What Cadence handles

Your term (courses, timetable, evaluations, grades) and what you tell Cadence so it can help you, including the way you study. This information is used only to make Cadence work for you.

Test version

The test version sends no data to Cadence: the sample term and your settings stay on your phone.

The microphone

When you touch the orb, your phone’s speech recognition transcribes your sentence; it may go through the internet, depending on its settings. Cadence receives only the text, which stays on your phone, and never keeps your voice.

Your credentials

Your school access code and password stay on your phone; they are never sent to us.

Other calendars

If you connect another calendar, Cadence reads only what you choose and writes nothing to it.

Pre-order

Payment for a pre-order is processed by Stripe. Your name, student ID and email are asked there, only to activate the month in your name and to refund you on request. Your optional answers on the pre-order page (how you found us, your association) are sent to Stripe with your payment, only to know how you found us; an association only sees totals.

No sale, no advertising

We sell no information and share none for advertising. Cadence shows no ads.

Retention

Your information is kept while you use Cadence and erased on request, except what the law requires us to keep, such as the record of a payment.

Security

What Cadence sends is encrypted in transit.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information: write to joram@adminva.ca. In the test version, uninstalling the app erases what it keeps on your phone; see also the Account deletion page.

Age

Cadence is meant for adult post-secondary students, 18 and over. It is not directed to children.

Changes

A significant change to this policy is announced in the app before it takes effect.

Your whole term. You speak, it follows.
