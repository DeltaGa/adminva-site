Support

A question, a problem, a request about your personal information?

Write to joram@adminva.ca, subject “Cadence”. Answer within two business days.

Common questions

I don’t have a student account.
On the first screen, “Essayer avec une session d’exemple” (try a sample term) opens Cadence on a complete fictitious term.
My password changed.
Cadence notices and invites you to sign in again in one screen; nothing of your term is lost.
I lost access to my authenticator app.
Your school’s IT support can reset it; Cadence cannot do it for you.
Manage or cancel my subscription.
In your app store settings (Google Play or App Store).
Delete my account.
See the Account deletion page.

Your whole term. You speak, it follows.
