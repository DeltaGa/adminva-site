Terms of use

Effective November 7, 2026. Cadence mobile app, published by Joram Smith Tchicdje Kouojip, Quebec, Canada. The French version prevails.

The service

Cadence imports your term (courses, timetable, course outlines, evaluations, published grades) from your student account and lets you manage it by voice or keyboard. Cadence is an independent app, not affiliated with your school. It acts on your phone, with your account, at your request, and does only what your school already offers you: view your timetable, your courses, your grades and download your course outlines.

Your student account

You remain responsible for your account and for following your school’s rules. Your credentials stay on your phone. You can remove at any time what Cadence keeps, in the settings.

Accuracy

Cadence reads what your school publishes and what your course outlines say. The official dates, weights and grades are those of your school and your teachers. When in doubt, the official source prevails. Cadence flags what it could not read with certainty.

Your evening plan

Cadence prepares your evening plan from your term and from what you tell it about the way you study. It is a proposal: you are free to follow it, change it or ignore it, and nothing you said changes without your confirmation.

Subscription and trial

Cadence is offered by subscription, with a free trial for new subscribers, at the prices shown in the app and by your app store (App Store or Google Play). Payment, automatic renewal and cancellation are handled by the store, under its terms. You can cancel at any time in your store settings; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

Permitted use

Personal use only. You may not use Cadence to access another person’s account, to get around its usage limits or to disrupt your school’s services.

Shared course sheet

A change you choose to share with your section (for instance, a new room) is offered to the other students of your section who use Cadence, without your name. Share only what is accurate.
